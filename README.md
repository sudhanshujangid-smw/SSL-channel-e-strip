## 2. Saturation & Analog Color Architecture (Phase 8)

Implemented in [`Source/DSP/Saturation.h`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/DSP/Saturation.h) and [`Source/DSP/Saturation.cpp`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/DSP/Saturation.cpp) with dedicated visual transfer curve and activity metering in [`Source/GUI/SaturationMeter.h`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/GUI/SaturationMeter.h) and [`Source/GUI/SaturationMeter.cpp`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/GUI/SaturationMeter.cpp):

### Mathematical Specifications & Processing
1. **Original Continuous-Morphing Nonlinear Waveshaper:**
   - **Drive ($0\% \to 100\%$):** Smooth logarithmic mapping from $0.0\text{ dB}$ ($1.0\times$) up to $+36.0\text{ dB}$ ($63.1\times$).
   - **Perceptual Loudness Compensation:** Automatic internal gain compensation $G_{\text{comp}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{1 + 0.35 \cdot (G_{\text{drive}} - 1.0)}}$ preserves perceived loudness so extreme drive introduces rich saturation texture rather than harsh volume spikes.
   - **Character Morphing ($0\% \to 100\%$):**
     * **Asymmetry Factor ($A = (1 - c) \cdot 0.32$):** Positive excursions are smoothly expanded/compressed via quadratic term $x_a = x + A \frac{x^2}{1 + |x|}$, producing rich 2nd-order even harmonics (warm tube/transformer tone) at low character that fades to pure odd symmetry at $100\%$.
     * **Morphing Algebraic Sigmoid:** Curvature exponent $p = 1.25 + 1.75 \cdot c$:
       $$y = \frac{x_a}{(1 + |x_a|^p)^{1/p}}$$
       Provides silky, soft-knee tape saturation ($c \approx 50\%$) to aggressive, articulate console bus overdrive ($c = 100\%$) without digital wrap-around or harsh clipping edges.
2. **Anti-Harshness Analog HF Conditioning:**
   - 1-pole low-pass pre-conditioning filter at 19 kHz prevents ultrasonic intermodulation and brittle high-frequency fizz, creating an authentic warm analog texture.
3. **Sample-Exact DC-Blocking High-Pass Filter:**
   - 1-pole high-pass DC blocker at 10 Hz ($R \approx 0.9985$ at 44.1 kHz): $y[n] = x[n] - x[n-1] + R \cdot y[n-1]$. Completely removes any DC offset generated by asymmetric waveshaping with zero phase distortion in the audible band.
4. **Phase-Coherent Parallel Dry/Wet Blend:**
   - $s_{\text{blended}}[n] = (1 - \text{mix}) \cdot s_{\text{dry}}[n] + \text{mix} \cdot s_{\text{wet}}[n]$. Zero latency ensures zero comb-filtering across all mix positions.
5. **Linear Output Trim & 15 ms Clickless Bypass:**
   - Output level adjustment from $-12\text{ dB}$ to $+12\text{ dB}$ smoothed over 25 ms, coupled with a 15 ms master bypass crossfade.
6. **Dynamic Waveshaper Scope & LED Ladder:**
   - Visual transfer curve displays real-time morphing of the waveshaper curve, dynamic excursion dot/glow, mode indicator (`WARM TUBE`, `ANALOG TAPE`, `CONSOLE DRIVE`), and 4-segment saturation activity LED ladder (`WARM`, `DRIVE`, `COLOR`, `CRUNCH`).

## 3. Compressor Architecture (Phase 7)

Implemented in [`Source/DSP/Compressor.h`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/DSP/Compressor.h) and [`Source/DSP/Compressor.cpp`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/DSP/Compressor.cpp) with dedicated visual metering in [`Source/GUI/CompressorGRMeter.h`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/GUI/CompressorGRMeter.h) and [`Source/GUI/CompressorGRMeter.cpp`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/GUI/CompressorGRMeter.cpp):

### Mathematical Specifications & Ballistics
1. **Continuous Quadratic Soft-Knee Characteristic:**
   - Threshold $T \in [-60\text{ dB}, 0\text{ dB}]$, Ratio $R \in [1.0:1, 20.0:1]$, Knee width $W \in [0\text{ dB}, 24\text{ dB}]$.
   - Below knee ($x_G < T - W/2$): Unattenuated linear pass-through ($y_G = x_G$).
   - Within knee ($T - W/2 \le x_G \le T + W/2$): Smooth $C^1$-continuous parabolic curve:
     $$y_G = x_G + \frac{(1/R - 1)(x_G - T + W/2)^2}{2W}$$
   - Above knee ($x_G > T + W/2$): Fixed compression ratio slope:
     $$y_G = T + \frac{x_G - T}{R}$$
   - Target gain reduction: $GR_{\text{target}} = y_G - x_G \le 0.0\text{ dB}$.
2. **Linked Stereo Peak Detection:**
   - Detects the linked peak $x_{\text{sc}} = \max(|s_L[n]|, |s_R[n]|)$ with a $-100\text{ dB}$ anti-denormal floor to maintain perfect stereo imaging and eliminate center-channel wandering.
3. **Log-Domain Ballistics Smoother:**
   - Operates directly in decibels on the gain reduction curve to ensure constant perceptual dB/second attack and decay rates regardless of signal magnitude.
   - Attack time $\tau_{\text{att}} \in [0.1\text{ ms}, 100\text{ ms}]$: $\alpha_{\text{att}} = 1 - e^{-2.2 / (\tau_{\text{att}} \cdot f_s)}$.
   - Release time $\tau_{\text{rel}} \in [5\text{ ms}, 2000\text{ ms}]$: $\alpha_{\text{rel}} = 1 - e^{-2.2 / (\tau_{\text{rel}} \cdot f_s)}$.
4. **Adaptive Program-Dependent Auto-Release:**
   - When `compressorAutoRelease` is engaged, a dual-time-constant network tracks short-term transient spikes versus sustained signal body.
   - A leaky 400 ms RMS/peak envelope tracks background signal density.
   - For fast transient peaks (snare hits, vocal plosives), release adapts quickly (~50 ms) to avoid unnatural pumping and breathing.
   - For sustained bass notes, heavy vocal belts, or dense mix program material, release smoothly extends up to 750 ms, preventing low-frequency intermodulation distortion.
5. **Phase-Coherent Parallel Compression (`compressorMix`):**
   - Sample-accurate linear blending: $s_{\text{out}}[n] = (1 - \text{mix}) \cdot s_{\text{dry}}[n] + \text{mix} \cdot s_{\text{comp}}[n]$.
   - Zero-latency DSP engine guarantees zero comb filtering or phase smearing across any wet/dry mix ratio (0% to 100%).
6. **Smoothed Linear Makeup Gain (`compressorMakeup`):**
   - Ranging from $-12\text{ dB}$ to $+24\text{ dB}$, smoothed over 20 ms to prevent zipper artifacts during automation.
7. **15 ms Clickless Master Bypass Crossfade:**
   - Master bypass toggling initiates a 15 ms linear crossfade (`bypassFade`) to prevent audio pops.
8. **Calibrated Gain Reduction Metering:**
   - Downward LED meter displaying calibrated points: `0`, `-3`, `-6`, `-10`, `-20`, and `-30` dB.
   - Audio thread safely posts reduction values via `std::atomic<float>` without mutexes or locks.

## 3. Gate / Expander Architecture (Phase 6)

Implemented in [`Source/DSP/GateExpander.h`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/DSP/GateExpander.h) and [`Source/DSP/GateExpander.cpp`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/DSP/GateExpander.cpp) with dedicated visual metering in [`Source/GUI/GateGRMeter.h`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/GUI/GateGRMeter.h) and [`Source/GUI/GateGRMeter.cpp`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/GUI/GateGRMeter.cpp):

### Mathematical Specifications & Ballistics
1. **Dual Operating Modes (`gateMode`):**
   - **Gate:** Hard/soft gating with a 2.0 dB soft-knee transition zone to eliminate boundary chatter. Below threshold, attenuation drops cleanly towards `-rangeDb`. Above threshold, gain opens to unity ($0.0\text{ dB}$).
   - **Expander:** Musical downward expander with a $1:2.5$ downward expansion ratio ($1.5\text{ dB}$ attenuation per $1\text{ dB}$ below threshold), smoothly bounded at `-rangeDb`. Preserves room ambiance and vocal decay tails while attenuating background noise.
2. **Stereo-Linked Peak Detection:**
   - Detector responds to the linked stereo peak level $x_{\text{sc}} = \max(|s_L[n]|, |s_R[n]|)$ with a $-100\text{ dB}$ floor to avoid denormals. Prevents stereo image collapse or one-sided chatter.
3. **Attack, Hold, and Release State Machine:**
   - **Attack (0.1 ms – 100 ms):** Ultra-fast exponential opening ($\alpha_{\text{att}} = 1 - e^{-2.2 / (\tau_{\text{att}} \cdot f_s)}$) capturing transient snare hits and vocal consonants without clipping.
   - **Hold (0 ms – 500 ms):** Sample-exact hold timer preventing the gate from prematurely closing during brief pauses between words or notes.
   - **Release (5 ms – 2000 ms):** Smooth exponential closing ($\alpha_{\text{rel}} = 1 - e^{-2.2 / (\tau_{\text{rel}} \cdot f_s)}$) for natural decay without pumping or clicks.
4. **Dedicated Sidechain Filtering:**
   - High-Pass Filter ($20\text{ Hz} - 2000\text{ Hz}$, 2nd-order Butterworth biquad).
   - Low-Pass Filter ($1000\text{ Hz} - 20000\text{ Hz}$, 2nd-order Butterworth biquad).
   - Filters isolate detector sensitivity (e.g. cutting low rumble or high bleed) without affecting the main audio path.
5. **15 ms Clickless Master Bypass Crossfade:**
   - Engaging or disengaging the gate ramps a 15 ms linear crossfade (`bypassFade`), completely eliminating pop artifacts. Fast-path returns immediately when bypassed.
6. **Calibrated Gain Reduction Metering:**
   - Downward LED meter with calibrated markings at `0 dB`, `-5 dB`, `-10 dB`, `-20 dB`, `-40 dB`, and `-60 dB`.
   - Real-time thread-safe atomic communication between audio thread and GUI timer.

## 8. VST3 Installation & FL Studio Testing

### Installation
Run the included PowerShell installer script:
```powershell
.\install_vst3.ps1 -Config Release
```
This installs `SJ Channel Strip.vst3` into:
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\VST3\SJ Channel Strip.vst3`

### Testing in FL Studio
1. Open **FL Studio** and perform a plugin scan (**Options > Manage Plugins > Find installed plugins**).
2. Insert `SJ Channel Strip` on a mixer track with audio playing (drums, vocals, guitars, or full mix).
3. **Compressor Testing:**
   - Click **COMP ON** (green LED engages).
   - Sweep `THRESHOLD` from 0 dB down to -30 dB, -40 dB: observe downward gain reduction needle and LED meter responding smoothly.
   - Adjust `RATIO` from 1:1 up to 20:1: confirm aggressive peak limiting at high ratios and gentle transparent leveling at 2:1–4:1.
   - Adjust `ATTACK` (0.1 ms to 50 ms): fast attack clamps snare transients; slower attack (10–30 ms) lets drum punch punch through.
   - Adjust `RELEASE` (5 ms to 2000 ms): fast release adds density and energy; slow release smoothly stabilizes dynamic vocals without pumping.
   - Sweep `KNEE` (0 dB hard knee to 24 dB ultra-soft knee): soft knee eases compression onset around threshold.
   - Toggle **AUTO REL**: observe natural adaptive release on dynamic vocals and drum beats.
   - Adjust `MAKEUP` (-12 dB to +24 dB): compensate for gain reduction and level match.
   - Adjust `MIX` (0% to 100%): blend heavy punchy compression with transparent dry transients without any phase cancellation.
4. **Gate / Expander Testing:**
   - Click **GATE ON**. Set mode to **GATE** or **EXPANDER**.
   - Sweep `THRESHOLD` (-80 dB to 0 dB) and `RANGE` (0 dB to 80 dB).
   - Test sidechain HPF/LPF filtering on drum bleed.
5. **Gain Reduction Meters:**
   - Both Gate and Compressor GR meters provide dedicated down-facing LED ladders with calibrated markings (0, -3, -6, -10, -20, -30 dB for comp) and numeric readouts.
6. **Automation & Persistence:**
   - Automate `compressorThreshold`, `compressorRatio`, `compressorMakeup`, and `compressorMix`.
   - Save FL Studio project (.flp), reload, and confirm all 55 parameter values and states return intact.

### 1. Compressor Sidechain Architecture
- **Dedicated Detector Buffer:** Pre-allocated zero-allocation detector memory isolates the detection signal from the audio path.
- **Internal Filtering (HPF & LPF):**
  - **HPF:** 20 Hz to 2000 Hz (default 80 Hz), 2nd-order Butterworth biquad filter.
  - **LPF:** 1000 Hz to 20000 Hz (default 20000 Hz), 2nd-order Butterworth biquad filter.
  - **Filter Enable (`SC FILTER`):** Dedicated toggle button. When disabled, full bandwidth triggers the detector.
  - **Pristine Main Audio:** Sidechain filtering operates exclusively on detector memory; the primary program audio is never frequency-filtered by the sidechain filters.
- **Audition Mode (`LISTEN`):**
  - Toggles audition mode with a high-visibility amber status LED.
  - Routes the filtered sidechain detector audio directly to the main outputs so mixing engineers can precisely isolate trigger frequencies (e.g., kick punch or snare crack) before engaging compression.

### 2. External Sidechain Multi-Bus Architecture
- **JUCE Multi-Bus Integration:**
  - Registered secondary auxiliary input bus: `.withInput("Sidechain", juce::AudioChannelSet::stereo(), true)`.
  - Fully compliant with VST3 multi-bus standards and FL Studio wrapper requirements.
- **Dynamic Bus Negotiation:**
  - `isBusesLayoutSupported()` handles disabled, mono, or stereo sidechain configurations dynamically.
- **Graceful Fallback:**
  - The plugin never crashes if an external sidechain is unrouted, silent, or disabled by the host.
  - If `SC SRC` is set to `EXT` but no external signal is provided, it seamlessly falls back to the internal audio input.
- **Selectable Source (`SC SRC`):**
  - Independent source switching on both Compressor and Gate modules (`INT` = Internal main signal, `EXT` = External auxiliary sidechain bus).

### 3. Gate / Expander Sidechain Architecture
- **Detector Filtering:** Dedicated 2nd-order Butterworth HPF (20 Hz - 2000 Hz) and LPF (1000 Hz - 20000 Hz).
- **Source Selection & Audition:** Independent `SC SRC` (Internal / External) and `LISTEN` audition button.
- Perfect for surgical drum gating (e.g., keying the tom gate exclusively from the fundamental transient while rejecting cymbal bleed).

## 13. FL Studio Sidechain Routing Guide

Follow these steps to route an external track into the SJ Channel Strip sidechain in FL Studio:

```text
[Track 1: Kick Drum] ──(Sidechain Send)──> [Track 2: Synth / Bass with SJ Channel Strip]
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
                                          [Wrapper Processing Tab]
                                          "Auxiliary Inputs" -> "Sidechain" = 1 (Track 1)
                                                    │
                                                    ▼
                                          [SJ Channel Strip GUI]
                                          Compressor SC SRC: EXT
```

### Step-by-Step Instructions:
1. **Open FL Studio Mixer (`F9`)**:
   - Locate the **Trigger Source Track** (e.g., Track 1: Kick Drum).
   - Locate the **Target Track** (e.g., Track 2: Bass / Synth) where **SJ Channel Strip** is inserted.
2. **Create the Mixer Sidechain Link**:
   - Left-click on **Track 1 (Kick)** to select it.
   - Look at the bottom of **Track 2 (Bass)**. Right-click the small arrow / send icon at the bottom of Track 2.
   - Choose **"Sidechain to this track"** from the context menu.
   - *(Note: Ensure the send knob at the bottom of Track 2 is turned up to 100% / 0 dB so audio is passed to the sidechain bus without being heard on the main mix).*
3. **Configure FL Studio VST3 Wrapper**:
   - Click on **SJ Channel Strip** on Track 2 to open the plugin GUI.
   - In the top-left corner of the plugin window, click the **Gear Icon** (Detailed Settings).
   - Click the **Processing Tab** (icon resembling a plug with a gear).
   - Look at the **"Connections"** section on the right side.
   - Under **"Auxiliary Inputs"**, you will see **"Sidechain"**.
   - Right-click the box next to **"Sidechain"** and select **Track 1 (Kick)**, or type `1`.
4. **Engage Sidechain in SJ Channel Strip**:
   - Return to the **SJ Channel Strip** main interface (click the plug icon next to the gear).
   - In the **COMPRESSOR** section:
     - Change the **`SC SRC`** dropdown from **`INT`** to **`EXT`**.
     - Turn on **`COMP ON`**.
     - Set your desired Threshold (e.g. `-18 dB`) and Ratio (e.g. `4:1`).
5. **Tune the Sidechain Filter & Audition**:
   - Click **`SC FILTER`** to enable the sidechain bandpass filter.
   - Click **`LISTEN`** (turns amber). You will now hear the kick drum sidechain signal soloed in your monitors!
   - Adjust **`SC HPF`** (e.g. 80 Hz) and **`SC LPF`** (e.g. 2500 Hz) to focus the detector exclusively on the kick transient.
   - Click **`LISTEN`** again to disengage audition mode.
   - Play your track: The bass will now pump and duck cleanly whenever the kick drum hits, with zero clicks, phase issues, or crashes!

## 14. SJ Dynamic EQ Architecture (Priority 1)

Implemented in [`Source/DSP/DynamicEQ.h`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/DSP/DynamicEQ.h) and [`Source/DSP/DynamicEQ.cpp`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/DSP/DynamicEQ.cpp) with dedicated visual metering in [`Source/GUI/DynamicEQMeter.h`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/GUI/DynamicEQMeter.h) and [`Source/GUI/DynamicEQMeter.cpp`](file:///c:/Users/bhoom/OneDrive/Documents/SSL%20channel%20e%20strip/Source/GUI/DynamicEQMeter.cpp):

### Mathematical Specifications & DSP Design
1. **Parametric Peaking Filter with Dynamic Gain Modulation:**
   - 2nd-order Audio EQ Cookbook peaking (bell) biquad filter with variable center frequency ($20\text{ Hz} \to 20\text{ kHz}$), Q ($0.1 \to 10.0$), and static gain ($-18\text{ dB} \to +18\text{ dB}$).
   - Total effective gain $G_{\text{eff}} = G_{\text{static}} + G_{\text{dyn}}$, smoothed on a 16-sample sub-block grid to eliminate zipper artifacts while consuming $< 0.1\%$ CPU.
2. **Dedicated Sidechain Detector Bandpass Filter:**
   - 2nd-order constant 0 dB peak gain bandpass filter tracking the target frequency and Q precisely.
   - Detects the linked stereo peak level within the target frequency band.
3. **Log-Domain Ballistics Engine:**
   - Calibrated Threshold $T \in [-60\text{ dB}, 0\text{ dB}]$, Range $R \in [-24\text{ dB}, +24\text{ dB}]$.
   - Attack time $\tau_{\text{att}} \in [0.1\text{ ms}, 100\text{ ms}]$, Release time $\tau_{\text{rel}} \in [5\text{ ms}, 2000\text{ ms}]$.
4. **Dual Dynamic Processing Modes:**
   - **Compress (Cut):** Attenuates resonant or harsh frequency buildup when the band level exceeds threshold (ideal for vocal de-essing, boxiness cleanup, and harsh cymbal tame).
   - **Expand (Boost):** Upward dynamic expansion enhancing transient punch or vocal presence only when active.
5. **Acoustic Audition Mode (`LISTEN`):**
   - Solos the sidechain bandpass detector signal to allow rapid acoustic isolation of sibilance or offending resonances.
6. **Live Multi-Bar Metering:**
   - Real-time dynamic action bar (amber downward gain reduction / cyan upward boost) + detector level meter with red threshold needle.
